# 8 — Missing factors I: earnings quality, accounting risk, management & capital allocation

Research date 2026-09-22. Scope: what a rigorous rating should weigh on this lens that the built
system does not, with the evidence, the formula, the data the FactPack has or lacks, and where each
factor belongs in the 6.md architecture (gate / modulator / sleeve).

## Executive summary

1. The system's only earnings-quality read is Sloan cash-flow accruals on an invested-capital proxy
   (`gates.ts`). The literature's stronger, cheaper signals — **cash-conversion trend, working-capital
   accruals, and the non-GAAP-to-GAAP gap** — are absent, and two are computable from rows already in
   the pack.
2. **SBC is the single largest omission.** Every FCF figure in the pipeline adds SBC back; for a
   semiconductor/software book (AMD, NVDA, ORCL, CRWV, AVGO) that overstates owner earnings by
   5-20 % of revenue and inflates every reverse-DCF fair value. One XBRL concept fixes it.
3. **Net dilution** (share issuance net of buybacks) is unscored; the Piotroski "no new shares"
   proxy is a coarse stand-in. The pack has the buyback row but no share-count series.
4. **Capital-allocation quality** is half-built: incremental ROIC and the structural-break test exist
   (3.md), but goodwill impairments, buyback price discipline (repurchasing above the model's fair
   value) and capex-vs-D&A are not read.
5. **Governance is unread even though the DEF 14A is already in the pack** (`context.proxyStatement`).
   Pay-for-performance metrics, say-on-pay support, insider ownership, dual-class status and
   related-party items are mechanically extractable. Form 4 insider trades are a keyless fetch away.
6. Fraud/manipulation predictors (**Beneish M, Dechow F**) have real evidence (≈ −1 %/month for
   high-M names, risk-adjusted) but need receivables, total assets, SG&A and D&A — five XBRL concepts.
7. Placement: going-concern / material-weakness / high-M → **gates**; dilution and buyback-above-FV →
   **bear-floor / conviction modulators**; cash conversion and SBC-adjusted yield → **composite sleeves**.
8. Highest value for effort: **persist `ShareBasedCompensation` and the diluted share count**, then
   SBC-adjust owner earnings in `intrinsic.ts` and add net dilution as a conviction modulator.

---

## 1. Earnings quality beyond Sloan accruals

**Evidence.** Sloan (1996) shows the accrual component of earnings is less persistent than the cash
component and that low-accrual firms earn positive abnormal returns; the effect survived two decades
of scrutiny though it has attenuated post-2000 in large caps ([NBER w13525](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w13525/w13525.pdf),
[Quantpedia summary](https://quantpedia.com/strategies/accrual-anomaly)). Beneish's M-score identified
manipulators out-of-sample and high-M firms underperformed by just under 1 %/month risk-adjusted,
surviving size/value/momentum/accrual/short-interest controls; Dechow–Ge–Larson–Sloan (2011) F-score
uses seven indicators across accrual quality, performance, non-financial, off-balance-sheet and
market measures ([CFA UK review](https://www.cfauk.org/pi-listing/man-machine-the-evolution-of-fundamental-scoring-models-and-ml-implications)).

| Signal | Formula | Pack today | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cash conversion level & trend | `OCF/NI`, `FCF/NI`, 5-yr slope | ✅ computable (`operatingCashFlow`, `freeCashFlow`, `netIncome`) | — |
| Working-capital accruals (Sloan proper) | `(ΔCA − ΔCash) − (ΔCL − ΔSTDebt) − D&A`, scaled by avg total assets | ❌ | `AssetsCurrent`, `LiabilitiesCurrent`, `DepreciationDepletionAndAmortization`, `Assets` |
| Receivables outrunning revenue | `%ΔAR − %ΔRevenue` (Beneish DSRI) | ❌ | `AccountsReceivableNetCurrent` |
| Deferred-revenue trend (software) | `ΔContractLiability / Revenue` | ❌ | `ContractWithCustomerLiabilityCurrent` |
| Non-GAAP gap | `(nonGAAP EPS − GAAP EPS)/|GAAP EPS|`, trend | partial — press release text in `context.pressRelease` | LLM extraction with the desk's grounding rules; or `dei`-less; no XBRL |
| One-time item frequency | count of "restructuring/impairment/gain on sale" quarters | ❌ | `RestructuringCharges`, `GoodwillImpairmentLoss`, `AssetImpairmentCharges` |
| Effective-tax-rate volatility | σ of `TaxExpense/PretaxIncome` | ❌ | `IncomeTaxExpenseBenefit`, `IncomeLossFromContinuingOperationsBeforeIncomeTaxes...` |
| Beneish M-score (8 vars) | DSRI, GMI, AQI, SGI, DEPI, SGAI, LVGI, TATA | ❌ | receivables, total assets, PP&E, D&A, SG&A, current assets/liabilities |
| Dechow F-score | 7 indicators | ❌ | as above + soft assets, share issuance |

**Why the current accruals read is weak.** `(NI − OCF)/avg(equity+debt)` treats *all* non-cash
items as accruals and scales by a financing-side base. NVDA flagged HIGH on it during a receivables
build the gate then had to be told to ignore (5.md worked example). Sloan's working-capital
definition would separate a demand-driven receivables build from a discretionary one, and the
DSRI ratio would flag receivables growing *faster than revenue* — the actual red flag.

## 2. SBC and dilution as a real cost

**Evidence.** Damodaran's position — treat SBC as a cash expense, never add it back — is the
mainstream valuation view ([Musings on Markets](https://aswathdamodaran.blogspot.com/2014/02/stock-based-employee-compensation-value.html),
[Morgan Stanley Counterpoint Global](https://www.morganstanley.com/im/publication/insights/articles/article_stockbasedcompensation.pdf)).
Large-cap software SBC averages 15-18 % of revenue; at 10× sales, ignoring it inflates value by
1.5-2.5 turns, ~15-25 % ([Financial Edge](https://www.fe.training/free-resources/portfolio-management/stock-based-compensation-in-a-dcf-model/)).
A semiconductor-specific case study reaches the same conclusion ([JAF](https://articlegateway.com/index.php/JAF/article/view/7509)).

**What this does to the pipeline today.** `intrinsic.ts` capitalises trailing FCF with SBC added back
(4.md §3 flags it). For AMD (SBC ≈ $1.4 B on $8.4 B FCF) owner earnings fall ~17 % and the implied
growth the price requires rises from ~31 % to ~35 % — 4.md's own worked number. For CRWV/ORCL the
sign of the read does not change (both abstain); for NVDA/AVGO/LRCX the MoS worsens by 10-20 pts.

| Signal | Formula | Pack | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| SBC / revenue, SBC / FCF | levels and 5-yr trend | ❌ | `ShareBasedCompensation` (cash-flow statement add-back) |
| SBC-adjusted owner earnings | `FCF − SBC` (optionally − maintenance capex delta) | ❌ | same |
| Diluted share-count trend | `Δ WeightedAverageDilutedShares` | ❌ (only `NI/EPS` proxy) | `WeightedAverageNumberOfDilutedSharesOutstanding` |
| Net dilution | `Δshares + (buybacks / price)` net of issuance | partial (`buybacks` row) | share count |
| Buyback price discipline | share of buyback $ executed above the model's base FV | ✅ buybacks; ✅ base FV (4.md) | quarterly buyback + avg price (`StockRepurchasedDuringPeriodValue/Shares`) |
| Option/warrant overhang | (warrants + unvested RSUs) / shares | ❌ (AMD's OpenAI/Meta warrants live only in `context` text) | `ClassOfWarrantOrRightOutstanding`, `ShareBasedCompensationArrangement...NonvestedNumber` |

**Placement.** SBC-adjusted owner earnings is a *correction* to the intrinsic layer, not a new
factor. Net dilution > 3 %/yr is a **conviction modulator** and, above ~8 %, a gate-style **cap at
HOLD** (5.md's Piotroski signal 7 becomes redundant). Buybacks executed materially above the base
fair value are a capital-allocation demerit (a negative modulator on the moat's "durability" claim).

## 3. Management & capital allocation

The moat engine already reads incremental ROIC and detects stock-funded M&A (3.md §2). Missing:

| Signal | Formula | Pack | Needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goodwill impairments | impairment / goodwill, frequency | partial (`goodwill[]` now persisted — a *decline* between years is a proxy) | `GoodwillImpairmentLoss` |
| Capex discipline | `capex / D&A`, capex/revenue vs 5-yr mean | ❌ D&A | `DepreciationDepletionAndAmortization` |
| Return on incremental invested capital | ✅ `incrementalRoic` | ✅ | — |
| Shareholder yield vs FCF | `(buybacks + dividends) / FCF` | ✅ rows exist | — |
| Leverage choice | ND/EBITDA trend vs coverage | ✅ | — |
| M&A price discipline | goodwill created / deal EV; post-deal incremental ROIC | partial | deal disclosures (text) |
| CEO/CFO tenure & turnover | years; changes in 24 m | ❌ (proxy text) | DEF 14A extraction |
| Insider buying / selling | net open-market $ over 6-12 m, cluster buys | ❌ | **Form 4** (EDGAR full-text/`ownership` feed, keyless); FMP `insiderTrades` connector exists |

**Evidence.** Insider *purchases* (especially clustered, by multiple officers) carry modest but
robust predictive power for 6-12-month returns; sales are noisy (liquidity, diversification). Goodwill
impairments predict lower subsequent returns and signal prior overpayment.

## 4. Governance red flags — most are already in the pack's proxy statement

`context.proxyStatement` (DEF 14A) is captured for every report (FactPack 1.2). A grounded LLM
extraction pass — the desk already runs one for the report prose — can produce a small structured
`governance` block:

| Item | Where | Mechanical rule |
|---|---|---|
| Pay-for-performance metrics & TSR peer group | DEF 14A CD&A | flag if ≥ 50 % of LTI vests on non-GAAP/adjusted metrics only |
| Say-on-pay support | DEF 14A / 8-K item 5.07 | < 70 % → conviction demerit |
| Insider/officer ownership % | DEF 14A beneficial-ownership table | < 1 % (ex-founders) → demerit; > 10 % → alignment credit |
| Dual-class / controlled company | DEF 14A, 10-K cover | flag (governance discount in the uncertainty tier, 11.md) |
| Related-party transactions | DEF 14A Item 404 | any material → flag |
| Auditor tenure / change, opinion | 10-K Item 9A / 8-K 4.01 | change with disagreement → **gate: cap HOLD** |
| Material weakness (SOX 404) | 10-K Item 9A | present → **gate: cap HOLD** |
| Going-concern language | 10-K notes ("substantial doubt") | present → **gate: cap STRONG SELL** (5.md already wants this; it is a text search on the primary document the pipeline already downloads) |

## 5. Worked illustration (from rows that exist)

**AMD (FY25 pack).** Buybacks −$1.32 B on $6.7 B FCF (shareholder yield ~20 % of FCF); `NI/EPS`
share proxy 1,641 M → 1,636 M (−0.3 %) — but that proxy cannot see the ~320 M OpenAI/Meta warrants
(≈ 20 % potential dilution) that sit only in the risk-factor text. Cash conversion OCF/NI = 1.78, FCF/NI
= 1.55 — strong and improving over five years (FY23 trough 1.95/1.31). A Sloan working-capital
accrual and a DSRI would need receivables and total assets that the pack lacks.

**What an SBC row would change.** Using AMD's disclosed SBC (~$1.4 B), owner earnings ≈ $7.0 B, the
price-implied growth rises ~4 pts and the base FV falls ~12 %. The published HOLD survives; the
intrinsic MoS goes from −60 % to roughly −65 %. For NVDA (SBC ≈ $4.7 B on $97 B FCF, ~5 %) the effect
is small; for CRWV/ORCL it is moot (abstain).

## 7. What to build next (ranked by value / effort)

1. **Persist `ShareBasedCompensation` and `WeightedAverageNumberOfDilutedSharesOutstanding`** via
   `facts:enrich` (two companyconcept fetches, same pattern as goodwill); SBC-adjust
   `ownerEarningsBase`; add net dilution to conviction. *Small effort, changes every semi/software FV.*
2. **Going-concern + material-weakness text gates** on the primary 10-K document the pipeline already
   downloads (`edgar-primary.html`): two regexes, two caps. *Trivial; closes 5.md's stated gap.*
3. **Cash-conversion trend** into the Quality sleeve (rows exist). *Trivial.*
4. **Proxy-statement governance block** via the existing grounded LLM pass (say-on-pay, ownership,
   dual-class, related-party). *Medium; feeds the uncertainty tier in 11.md.*
5. **Receivables + total assets + D&A + SG&A** rows → Sloan-proper accruals, DSRI, Beneish M as a
   gate. *Medium; also unblocks Altman Z (5.md).*
6. **Form 4 insider net buying** (keyless EDGAR) as a positive modulator. *Medium.*
